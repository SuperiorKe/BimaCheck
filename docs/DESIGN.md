## Problem Statement
One-day build for the Africa's Talking Insurtech Hackathon (Nairobi, 25 Jun 2026), optimized to win judge attention in a single live demo and to signal senior engineering judgment to hiring eyes (prize money is irrelevant). Builder is a solo backend engineer (Node/Python) with an existing WhatsApp automation tool (Mama Mboga) for informal vendors, so strong AT messaging + conversational state-machine experience.

The insurance problem: informal-sector members face slow, opaque claims. Insurers respond to fraud (KES ~11bn lost from Kenya's SHA fund in six months) by making everyone wait, so honest claimants pay the price for fraudsters. The build resolves both: honest claims pay out in seconds because a deterministic decision engine can safely auto-approve the clean ones; suspicious claims are held for human review with a defensible reason.

## What Makes This Cool
Real money lands on a judge's actual phone via an M-Pesa B2C disbursement in under 30 seconds, on a feature phone, over USSD. The fraud engine is invisible; what the room feels is an honest claimant getting paid instantly while a fraud twin gets caught and held with a plain-language reason. The "whoa" is cash moving live; the senior signal is that the decision is deterministic, auditable, and IRA-defensible.

## Product framing (settled)
- **Product:** hospi-cash (fixed-benefit hospital cash), a real microinsurance category that pays members a fixed cash benefit on a confirmed admission. NOT vague "microinsurance," NOT parametric.
- **Protagonist:** the honest claimant who gets paid in seconds. Fraud scoring is the MECHANISM; fast trustworthy payout is the PRODUCT. This aligns the build with the event's stated financial-inclusion objective.
- **Source of truth:** a (seeded) facility admission feed. The member files a claim over USSD, but the engine validates it against the provider-submitted admission record. Nothing pays on bare member self-report. This was the key correction from the independent cold read.
- **Position:** RegTech sold TO licensed insurers / SHA-style funds. Builder carries no risk, sells no policy, never underwrites.

## Constraints
- Single day, in-person, solo, build + live demo.
- Rails: Africa's Talking SMS/USSD/Voice/Airtime + M-Pesa (Daraja sandbox). Feature-phone users.
- AT staff on-site provision working sandbox USSD shortcodes + live variables reliably (confirmed). This de-risks live USSD.
- **M-Pesa product taxonomy (critical):** a *payout to a member* is **B2C** (`/mpesa/b2c/v1/paymentrequest`), NOT STK push. STK push is C2B collection (it pulls money FROM a phone and prompts for a PIN); it cannot disburse. B2C sends money TO the member, who receives a standard M-Pesa "You have received KES X" SMS with no PIN prompt. Naming this distinction out loud on stage is itself a senior signal; mislabeling STK as a payout is the classic tourist tell.
- Daraja sandbox B2C requires: initiator name, an encrypted security credential, and queue-timeout + result callback URLs. The B2C *result* arrives only via callback, and sandbox callbacks fail ~40% of the time. The recipient's M-Pesa "received" SMS fires from Safaricom regardless of your result callback, so the audience-visible part is more reliable than your own confirmation.
- The recipient MSISDN must be one of Safaricom's published Daraja sandbox test numbers; an arbitrary judge's real Safaricom line will not receive a sandbox B2C payment (there is no member self-registration in sandbox). The security credential is generated by RSA-encrypting the initiator password against Safaricom's sandbox public certificate. Both are setup chores, not code; do them first.
- Public callback URL must be a pre-provisioned HTTPS cloud box, not ngrok (Safaricom blocks tunnels).
- USSD handler must respond inside ~10s or AT kills the session.
- Data Protection Act 2019: use synthetic/seeded data only, no real PII on stage.

## Premises (agreed)
1. The protagonist is the honest claimant paid in seconds, not the insurer catching fraud. Fraud scoring is the mechanism.
2. The 90-second wedge is two contrasting claims on a real phone (clean -> paid; suspicious -> held), not a dashboard.
3. The hero fraud case is the geo/time-impossible duplicate: same member, same claim, two facilities 40km apart, same hour.
4. Flagged != denied. Suspicious routes to human review with reason + confidence. Never auto-deny, never carry risk.
5. USSD intake is must-ship core (Mama Mboga strength); it is both the legibility win and the proof of using AT's flagship rail.
6. (Revised post cold-read) The claim is validated against a seeded facility admission feed; no payout on bare self-report.
7. (Revised post cold-read) The engine is NAMED DETERMINISTIC RULES, not ML. Say so out loud. This is the real "ethical AI" answer for a regulated decision.

## Cross-Model Perspective (independent Claude subagent cold read)
The cold read steelmanned the build correctly (money on a judge's phone is the whole demo) and surfaced two decisive corrections, both adopted:
- **Kill black-box "AI/scoring" language.** Judges and senior hires probe exactly one thing: how does it decide? A magic score reads as tourism. Named deterministic rules each returning rule-name + plain reason is the senior artifact, and is defensible under questioning.
- **The self-report adjudication hole.** No real fund auto-disburses on a member's bare USSD say-so; every real flow has a provider-submitted encounter as the claim source. The framed-as-conservative wedge was actually the least regulatorily plausible part. Resolved by hospi-cash + seeded facility-confirmation feed (member stays the USSD protagonist; the engine validates against the provider record), rather than the subagent's fuller "facility submits, provider paid" version, which would have discarded the inclusion framing and the feature-phone moment.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Payout-first wedge (minimal viable)
Pull the M-Pesa B2C payout into the core, cut the dashboard to a closing beat, exactly two seeded claims. Highest visceral wow, leans hardest on AT + M-Pesa, smallest surface. Risk: puts the higher-setup, callback-dependent Daraja B2C sandbox into must-ship.

### Approach B: Pipeline with insurer dashboard (safest)
Core = USSD + scoring + dashboard + auto-SMS; payout stays a checkpointed stretch. No live-payment dependency in core, but the felt moment becomes the dashboard, the weakest payoff.

### Approach C: Trust/explainability layer (freshest)
Same engine; differentiator is plain-language reasons by SMS + one-tap "request review", optionally an AT Voice callback reading the decision aloud. Hits the "trust/transparency" gap and uses more AT rails. Risk: subtle to demo if not paired with a hard catch.

## Recommended Approach
**A + C hybrid (chosen):** payout-first spine (M-Pesa B2C disbursement in the core, dashboard demoted to a closing beat) PLUS the SMS plain-language reason string on every decision. The felt moment (money on a phone) and the senior signal (an explained, human-in-the-loop, deterministic decision) win the AT audience and a hiring eye at the same time. Overrides the earlier "payout = stretch" plan, justified by AT's confirmed reliable on-site sandbox.

### Components (build in this order, protect the demo path)
1. **USSD claim flow** — reuse the Mama Mboga AT + state-machine code. Inputs: member ID, claim type, facility code, (amount is fixed by hospi-cash benefit). **The USSD handler does NO Daraja network I/O: it validates, enqueues a decision job, and returns immediately ("Claim received, processing").** This is what decouples the ~10s USSD budget from the ~30s payout, and keeps the synchronous path microseconds.
2. **Seeded facility admission feed** — flat JSON/SQLite table of admission records: `member_id, facility_code, facility_lat, facility_lng, admitted_at`. The lat/lng (or a hardcoded facility distance lookup) is required for the geo rule. Source of truth for validation. Pre-seed the clean match and the fraud twin.
3. **Decision engine** — small ordered list of NAMED deterministic rules, each returning rule name + plain reason + confidence. Concrete thresholds (so the seed and the rule agree):
   - `admission_match` (member's claim matches a facility admission record)
   - `duplicate_in_window` (same member + same claim type within **30 minutes**)
   - `geo_time_impossible` (two facilities **>10 km apart** with **<30 min** between the two claims) <- hero rule; seed the twin at 40km / 22min
   - `amount_or_velocity` (over threshold / too many claims) — add only if time
   Clean + matched -> APPROVE. Any anomaly -> HOLD (never DENY). Runs inside the background decision job, not the USSD handler.
4. **Payout + notify (background job, not USSD handler)** — APPROVE: Daraja **B2C disbursement** to the member's (sandbox test) MSISDN; the member receives M-Pesa's "received KES X" SMS plus your app SMS "Paid KES X, claim #123, approved: admission confirmed, no anomalies." HOLD: app SMS "Claim #124 received, under review: matches a claim filed 40km away 22 min ago." Never auto-deny.
5. **Dashboard (closing beat only)** — one HTML page + a JSON endpoint: live table of claims, decision, rule fired, reason. Built last, cut first if time is short.

### Deliberately skip
ML / any model, login/auth, ORM, insurer multi-tenancy, real KYC, anything off the 90-second path. Pre-seed the fraud twin so it is never fat-fingered live.

### Stack
Node (reuse Mama Mboga), Africa's Talking USSD + SMS, Daraja **B2C** (sandbox) for disbursement, SQLite or flat seed file, plain HTML dashboard. Pre-provisioned HTTPS cloud box (Render/Railway/Fly) for the B2C result/timeout callbacks before the day. A simple in-process queue (or even a setImmediate/worker) is enough for the decision+payout job; no broker needed.

### Contingency (decide by ~3pm)
B2C sandbox is more setup and more callback-dependent than STK. If B2C will not cooperate in time, the documented fallback is to pivot the live money moment to **STK collection** ("member pays a KES 1 co-pay and is instantly confirmed covered"), which is the reliable Daraja path and keeps a visceral PIN-prompt moment, at the cost of dropping the literal "paid out" claim. Primary remains B2C payout (correct and senior); this is the safety net, not plan A.

## Success Criteria
- Live: a clean claim filed over USSD on a real handset triggers a Daraja B2C disbursement; the pre-registered sandbox phone shows an M-Pesa "received KES X" SMS plus the app's approval SMS in under ~30s.

- The decision is shown to be deterministic and auditable (named rule + reason), explicitly contrasted with black-box ML.
- Demo lands in <=90s with a recorded fallback ready if the sandbox misbehaves.
